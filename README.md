# PeachyPawz
🐾 PeachyPawz

A clearer story for every paw.

PeachyPawz is an AI-powered pet health intelligence platform that transforms fragmented health records into a meaningful, personalized story.

Instead of simply storing vet reports, medications, measurements, symptoms, and reminders, PeachyPawz helps pet parents understand:

What happened → What changed → What patterns exist → What may need attention

Through a longitudinal Health Timeline, personal baselines, deterministic analytics, evidence-backed AI insights, document intelligence, and conversational interaction, PeachyPawz turns raw pet data into meaningful health stories, personalized insights, and responsible next actions.

Built as an independent prototype for the PetOlife AI Code-a-Thon. 🐁
